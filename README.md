# 🛒 Superstore Performance Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview
This project provides a comprehensive, data-driven executive overview of Superstore's performance. The interactive Power BI dashboard analyzes sales, profitability, customer segments, product categories, and shipping logistics to empower strategic decision-making.

## 📊 Dashboard Sections & Key Insights
* **Executive Overview:** High-level KPIs including Total Sales of $2M, Total Profit of $304K, Total Orders of 5K, and a Profit Margin of 13%.
* **Product & Category Performance:** Deep dive into top-performing sub-categories, highlighting Phones as the Top Sub-Category.
* **Customer & Segment Analytics:** Analysis of customer behavior, revealing $469 AVG Order Value across 793 Total Customers.
* **Logistics & Shipping Analysis:** Evaluation of shipping modes showing Standard Class as the most used, with 3.97 Average Shipping Days.
* **Profitability & Discount Strategy:** Insights into an Average Discount of 15% and $157K in Total Loss Amount.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
* **Data Visualization & Analytics:** Power BI
* **Key Features:** Decomposition Tree, Key Influencers, Custom Tooltips.
